# 0.4.1 hotfix report (2026-11-21), from tester reports of 0.4.0

Candidate c441 (c437 + the getenv include fix + the opt-in NIC trace of c438-c440; version 0.4.1). SHA-256 `CF08F70BC81B9AECDF07265D81884B9BF26F4109C41243C6C7978F38634E6410`. Validator PASS, 0 ICALL FAIL / STUB HIT / crash in the test runs. Mods rebuilt with `iso_to_mod.py prepare-stack` (both mods enabled). All screenshots are real captures (kept privately).

| # | Item | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Solo flag sticks | **Reproduced and fixed.** Solo, Back, Versus showed the Solo screen on c440 (the title debug showed shell cheats `4194304`). Cause: `ShellGameInfo.setShellCheats(x - bit)` does not clear a bit; `clearShellCheats(mask)` does. All clears now use it: main-menu focus (boot, Back, after a match), right after `createGame()` for every row, `Game.bsf`, `startGame()`. Verified (both mods, 1920x1080 and 2560x1080): Solo, Back, Versus / Action / Melee each show their own screen (Versus with VS and the P2 box, Action with difficulty, Melee free-for-all); Solo again shows Solo; the third row is drawn on all four. Not covered by a run: a full Solo match, then Versus (the clear at game load is the same call). The profile can still store the bit while the Solo screen is open; it is cleared at the next main-menu focus, before any other row can use it. |
| 2 | Biollante tile on the Solo screen | **Not reproduced.** With both mods stacked (16:9 and 21:9, 1 s sampling, cursor on and off her slot, after re-entering, after the stuck-flag path) the third-row tile is drawn on the Solo screen like on Versus. Also tried the unstacked layouts (mods prepared one by one): then her row is missing or unreachable, but that does not match the photos either. Needed from the reporter: whether it happens right after a fresh start or only after visiting other screens; the 0.4.0 package's `data\logs` of that run; whether Prepare was run after enabling both mods. |
| 3 | Solo title font | **Fixed.** The retail title object fits its text into 175 px by shrinking it; the Solo mod now also raises that limit (`builtin:solo_ui` on ControlRoom.bdl, stacks with Biollante). Same font, size, colour and position as VERSUS (screenshot reg1/s1.png frames 198 and 204 vs 144). |
| 4 | Bios icon wrap | **Retail behaviour, left as is.** Retail monsters with four icons wrap the same way (Godzilla 2000 INFLICTS: the fourth icon, fire, sits under the row; Godzilla 90's the same, see the tester's own photo). Biollante's four RESISTS icons wrap identically. |
| 5 | Attract movie black | **Not reproduced.** Idle at the title, at the profile screen and at the main menu (after visiting Solo and back), 16:9 and 21:9, c437 and c440, none / one / both mods: INTRO, TITLE and the in-engine attract demos show their picture (only the fade frames at the start and end of each movie are black). Cannot test the c435 range hypothesis against a repro; if it is the front-end state test, the cause would show as a black movie right after a first boot too. Need: which movie (opening, title, a cut-scene), the resolution, the `ste_video.ini` of that run. |
| 6 | Globe at the right of the ultrawide main menu | **Not reproduced in the steady state.** After first boot, after Solo and Back, after Versus and Back at 2560x1080 the main-menu sides show the plain wall. The Back transition briefly pulls the camera back (frame 246 of the Solo-back run) and the tester's photo shows the transition wipe band, so the photo may be that moment; a persistent globe would mean the camera did not return. Need: the display size, whether it stays after a few seconds. |

Retail guard: with no mod enabled nothing changed (the mod edits only apply with the mods).
